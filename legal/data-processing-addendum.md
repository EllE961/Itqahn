# Data Processing Addendum (Template)

**Effective date:** 2026-01-06

> This template must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before use.

## 1. Roles
- Customer is typically the **Controller**.
- Provider is the **Processor** for Customer Data.

## 2. Processing
Provider processes Customer Data only to provide the Service and as documented in the Agreement.

## 3. Security Measures
Provider implements measures including:
- least privilege access controls
- encryption in transit and at rest
- vulnerability management
- incident response processes
- audit logs and Evidence Pack generation (for governance)

## 4. Subprocessors
Provider may use subprocessors. A list and notification mechanism should be defined.

## 5. Assistance
Provider will reasonably assist Customer with:
- data subject requests,
- security assessments,
- audits as agreed.

## 6. Retention and Deletion
Customer configures retention schedules. Provider deletes data according to the configured schedules and Agreement terms.

## 7. International Transfers
To be defined based on hosting and jurisdictions.

## 8. Audit
Audit procedures should be defined in the Agreement or SLA.

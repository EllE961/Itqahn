# Terms of Service (Template)

**Effective date:** 2026-01-06

> This template must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before use.

## 1. Definitions
- **Service**: the Itqahn platform, including portal, workflows, agents, and tooling.
- **Customer**: the entity using the Service.
- **User**: an individual authorized by Customer.
- **Output**: any code, configuration, documents, artifacts, or deployments produced by the Service.
- **Evidence Pack**: signed bundle of provenance, SBOM, scans, tests, and approvals for a release.

## 2. Eligibility & Account Responsibilities
Customer is responsible for:
- ensuring Users are authorized,
- maintaining accurate account information,
- enforcing least-privilege access in Customer org.

## 3. Acceptable Use
Customer and Users must comply with the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP).
Prohibited uses include (non-exhaustive):
- illegal activity, malware, phishing, exploitation,
- unauthorized access attempts,
- requests to bypass security controls, audits, or governance,
- creating or distributing harmful content.

- Provider does not guarantee Output is error-free, compliant, or fit for a particular purpose without Customer validation.

- Customer acknowledges that the Service may store Evidence Packs and operational logs to provide auditability.

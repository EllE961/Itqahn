---

## Governance Alignment

These documents align with Itqahn's governance model:

- **Evidence-first operations** — All changes backed by evidence
- **Policy-as-code enforcement** — OPA/Kyverno governance
- **Multi-tenant data handling** — Isolation and classification
- **Security disclosure** — Responsible vulnerability reporting
- **Responsible AI use** — Agent behavior constraints

---

## Customization

Before deployment:

1. Have legal counsel review all documents
2. Customize contact information and entity names
3. Adjust terms for your jurisdiction
4. Update version dates
5. Publish in appropriate locations

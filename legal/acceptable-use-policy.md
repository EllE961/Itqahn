# Acceptable Use Policy (Template)

**Effective date:** 2026-01-06

The Itqahn Service must not be used to:

1. Conduct illegal activity or facilitate wrongdoing.
2. Create or distribute malware, spyware, phishing content, or exploits.
3. Attempt unauthorized access to systems or data.
4. Bypass security controls, audits, or policy enforcement.
5. Generate or deploy Output that is designed to harm people, systems, or organizations.
6. Use the Service in a way that creates unreasonable risk to the platform or other tenants (including denial-of-wallet behavior).

Violations may result in suspension or termination.

This AUP is enforced via:
- intake filtering,
- tool allowlists,
- policy-as-code gates,
- and security monitoring.
